# Source ledger

Search and inspection date: **2 October 2026**. This is a bounded primary-source check, not a systematic priority certificate. No theorem was imported from an inaccessible full text. The current direct proof does not depend on the v1.1 singular-transfer manuscript.

## Model and established dynamics

**Wilhelm, T., & Heinrich, R. (1995).** Smallest chemical reaction system with Hopf bifurcation. *Journal of Mathematical Chemistry, 17*, 1–14. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01165134

Access: publisher metadata and abstract, supported by later primary accounts. Full original article not obtained. Used for model provenance, not an uninspected detailed theorem.

**Wilhelm, T., & Heinrich, R. (1996).** Mathematical analysis of the smallest chemical reaction system with Hopf bifurcation. *Journal of Mathematical Chemistry, 19*, 111–130. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01165179

Access: publisher metadata and abstract, supported by later primary accounts. Full original article not obtained. Local Hopf analysis is acknowledged; the current paper independently computes the coefficient it needs.

**Smith, H. L. (2012).** Global dynamics of the smallest chemical reaction system with Hopf bifurcation. *Journal of Mathematical Chemistry, 50*, 989–995. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10910-011-9946-9

Inspected author preprint: https://arxiv.org/html/1109.5204v1 (dated 23 September 2011).

Load-bearing uses: model scaling; competitive Poincaré–Bendixson property; permanence (Theorems 1.3 and 1.4); above-Hopf finiteness, existence and convergence (Theorem 1.1(c)). The preprint expressly leaves cycle number open. We do not infer uniqueness from convergence. The current paper gives its own periodic compactness and critical global-stability arguments.

**Boros, B., & Hofbauer, J. (2023).** Some minimal bimolecular mass-action systems with limit cycles. *Nonlinear Analysis: Real World Applications, 72*, 103839. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nonrwa.2023.103839

Inspected preprint: https://arxiv.org/html/2202.11034v2, especially Section 4, Theorem 13 and the paragraph following it.

Uses: independently checking which original-system conclusions were established, including supercritical Hopf and critical equilibrium stability; verifying that original cycle count is retained as a question in this account. Its mass-conserving extension is different and cannot supply a counterexample to the present model.

## Monotone-feedback context

**Mallet-Paret, J., & Smith, H. L. (1990).** The Poincaré–Bendixson theorem for monotone cyclic feedback systems. *Journal of Dynamics and Differential Equations, 2*, 367–421. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01054041

Access: official publisher record; the applicable Poincaré–Bendixson input was inspected through Smith's explicit model-specific statement. This source positions related geometric theory. The new paper does not claim that familiar planar-embedding or extrema-ordering properties are first discoveries; it proves those properties directly for its exact model.
