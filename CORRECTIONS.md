# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to wilhelm-heinrich-large-gain 3.1.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23126164)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v3.1.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/wilhelm-heinrich-large-gain/>.

1. Corrected the import inventory in §1.4, second paragraph. The archived text says Section 7 “uses only classical planar and invariant-manifold theory”. Section 7 also imports the Birkhoff–Hopf contraction theorem, the Jordan and Schoenflies theorems and Whitney's C¹ extension theorem, which are listed as (I1)–(I5) in Section 7.1. The sentence should read: “Section 7 proves dissipativity and the structure of the attractor directly, importing only the classical results (I1)–(I5) of Section 7.1.”

   The imported results are standard and were already listed in Section 7.1; only the summary is affected. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
